Meet your designer

About Dane

I'm Dane Dison, the designer behind Mighty Organix Consulting and a graduate of the University of Houston. I build websites and signs for small businesses around Columbia, Spring Hill, Franklin and Nashville. I keep things simple: a site that loads fast, looks right on a phone, and makes it easy for customers to call, book or pay.

I've been building websites since the early 2000s, when I built all the websites for Hanes Consulting Incorporated, on Waters Avenue in Tampa, and served as general manager of HCI Flash Tampa (hciflashtampa.com). Through Hanes Consulting, my Tampa web work reached into Ybor City: I designed and maintained about half of all the websites for the nightclubs on the Ybor City Strip, including Amphitheater Ybor, Prana and Masquerades. I've also taught scuba diving for 25 years, so I'm used to explaining new things in plain, simple steps.

You get one person from first call to launch, and a fixed quote before any work starts.
